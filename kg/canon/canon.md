# Canon

Open only the entries relevant to the task. Use approved pointers and source limits under [[kg/schema/knowledge-graph-schema]]. Placeholder nodes are scaffolds, not approved business truth.

- [[kg/canon/product-truth]]: what the product does, capability limits, and current implementation claims.
- [[kg/canon/trust-boundaries]]: pricing, security, privacy and guarantees; verify current supporting sources.
- [[kg/canon/icp]]: audience, buying conditions, fit and disqualifiers.
- [[kg/canon/category-pov]]: the organization's source-supported market thesis.
- [[kg/canon/positioning]]: category, offer, differentiation and boundaries.
- [[kg/canon/proof-map]]: evidence for material claims; preserve observation dates and limits.
- [[kg/canon/claim-labels]]: Grounded, Hypothesis and TBD treatment.
- [[kg/canon/voice]]: read before writing for the organization; use relevant approved examples.
- [[kg/canon/nomenclature]]: preferred terms and exact definitions.
