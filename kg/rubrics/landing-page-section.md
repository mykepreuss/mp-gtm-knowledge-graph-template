# Rubric: Landing page section (v1)

## Gates (fail if any are false)

- No unbounded claims without evidence.
- Pricing, privacy, and security claims are evidenced or labeled TBD.
- ICP signals are explicit (who it is for, who it is not).
- No hype language.

## Score (0 to 10)

Score each category 0, 1, or 2.

1) Clarity and specificity
2) Mechanism (not vibes)
3) Proof and honesty
4) ICP fit signaling
5) Voice match
