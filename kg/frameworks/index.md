# Frameworks

Reusable frameworks designed to be:

- structure-first (consistent sections and checklists)
- evidence-first (claims point to proof artifacts)
- AI-friendly (easy to retrieve, quote, and cite)

Open only when the task benefits from the structure: [[kg/frameworks/aeo-framework]] and [[kg/frameworks/aeo-query-set]] for answer/discovery research; [[kg/frameworks/zero-click-framework]] for distribution planning; [[kg/frameworks/capabilities-and-benefits-framework]] for explaining product value; [[kg/frameworks/jtbd-struggling-moment-framework]] for buyer context. A framework supplies a method, not verified market evidence or owner belief.
