# The Six Pillars of Zero-Click Marketing (Template)

AI answers create a zero-click reality: users often get what they need inside the chat. Your brand is evaluated across multiple micro-queries before a click happens.

Use this framework to:
- clarify what visibility means when clicks drop
- choose metrics that match the new journey
- prioritize proof surfaces that LLMs actually cite

## 1) AI answer presence

Definition: how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers for high-intent queries.

## 2) LLM referrals and brand lift

Definition: AI increases branded demand while decreasing low-intent clicks.

## 3) Entity clarity (entity AEO)

Definition: LLMs form an internal model of your entity: what you are, who you’re for, and where you fit.

## 4) Conversational commerce

Definition: buyers ask AI for comparisons, pricing, and recommendations and decide before visiting your site.

## 5) Blurred attribution

Definition: traditional analytics undercount influence when the decision happens in chat.

## 6) AI as primary reader

Definition: you have two audiences: AI systems and the humans AI endorses you to.
